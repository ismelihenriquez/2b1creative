Receipts

Anonymous case studies. Very non-anonymous results.

01

From ad-drain to cult following.

The Problem: The brand, an athletic apparel brand, was relying on paid ads that weren't producing sustainable results.

The Play: We shifted the focus from chasing cold traffic to building a system centered around community, retention, email marketing, customer feedback, and organic content that had already proven itself.

The Proof: +30,000 followers in 60 days. Website traffic grew from thousands per month to thousands per week. $3,000 in ad spend generated $19,436 in revenue. 40% of sales came from returning customers by month five.

The Result: A more profitable brand that relied less on constant ad spend and more on customer loyalty.

30k followers$19,436 revenue40% returning customers60 days
02

From solo operator to 6-person fleet.

The Problem: A local landscaping owner relied almost entirely on word-of-mouth referrals. He wanted to scale, but without predictable leads, hiring a crew felt too risky.

The Play: We built a hyper-local visibility engine: website optimization, AI chat agent, lead qualification, scheduling support, customer testimonial recovery, a free-service giveaway campaign, localized content, local ads, and Google Maps optimization.

The Proof: Consistent lead flow helped him hire his first full-time employee within 60 days. By Month 8, he purchased a second truck and hired again. By Month 15, he moved out of manual labor into a managerial role with 6 full-time employees and multiple trucks running daily.

First hire in 60 daysSecond truck6 employeesOwner moved into management
03

Pretty content finally got strategy.

The Problem: A boutique restaurant had professional footage, but the videos were not performing. The visuals looked good, but there was no hook strategy, story, or reason for local viewers to care.

The Play: We built a 30-day content system focused on stronger hooks, better storytelling, creative direction, and content designed specifically for the local audience.

The Proof: Organic engagement increased by over 300% in less than two weeks. By Day 13, the owners reported a surge in foot traffic and one of their busiest dining periods since launching the campaign.

300%+ engagementLess than 2 weeksDay 13 foot traffic surge
04

A dead list became a traffic flood.

The Problem: An established ecommerce brand had built an email list of more than 83,000 subscribers, but engagement was declining. Promotional email blasts were generating fewer results, unsubscribe rates were increasing, and a valuable audience was becoming less responsive with every campaign.

The Play: Instead of sending more sales emails, we rebuilt the customer experience around storytelling, value, conversation, and anticipation. We introduced more human copy, creative conversion-focused visuals, and a strategic one-week launch sequence designed to build excitement before the promotion ever went live.

The Proof: On launch day, the website received more than 32,000 unique visitors, with analytics showing that 65% of all traffic came directly from the email campaign. The launch generated $388,213 in sales, transforming an underperforming email list into the primary driver of traffic, engagement, and revenue.
